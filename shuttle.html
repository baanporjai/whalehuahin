Shuttle times.

Choose a direction to see every trip. Times are Hua Hin local time.

Remark

Please always reserve your seat in advance, at least 30 minutes before departure, to avoid mistakes. Without a reservation, the shuttle may not run at the listed times. Arrival times are estimates; the bus may arrive 10–20 minutes later if traffic is heavy.

How to ride.

  1. Reserve your seat

    Reserve at the lobby or by phone at least 30 minutes before the shuttle time.

    +66 95 283 4932 · +66 32 522 202

  2. Be there early

    Coming back, be at your pick-up point (City Center, Hua Hin Beach or Market Village) a few minutes before the pick-up time shown. Seats are reserved in advance only.

  3. Allow for traffic

    The shuttle can be 10–20 minutes late, depending on traffic.
