Three Easy Days in Hua Hin

A simple plan for a first visit.

Three unhurried days: a little town, a little sea and plenty of time by the pool. We are about 5 km from Hua Hin town and the free shuttle runs to the city center, so you can leave the driving behind. Reserve your seat in advance.

Day 1 · Town and sea

Late morning: take the free shuttle into town and have lunch at Guay Pochana, a Chinese-style eatery known for its Hong Kong noodles and crispy roast pork.

Afternoon: stroll to Hua Hin railway station, one of the town’s best-known landmarks, then walk along the beach once the sun softens.

Evening: dinner at the Hua Hin Night Market, then head back to the hotel. Check the last shuttle time before you go.

Day 2 · A slow day at Whale

Morning: breakfast, then the rooftop pool and slide, with a massage at Well Retreat if you feel like it.

Afternoon: a short outing to Khao Takiab, where monkeys greet you at the temple and the hilltop view looks out over the coast. Entry is free; keep food out of sight.

Evening: sunset drinks at the rooftop bar.

Day 3 · Wine, markets and a slow goodbye

Morning: choose one. Hua Hin Hills Vineyard for wine tasting and a quiet lunch among the vines, or, if you are travelling with children, a water park (Vana Nava Water Jungle or Black Mountain Water Park) or Hua Hin Safari & Adventure Park for animal shows and feeding.

Last evening: if your stay includes a Friday, Saturday or Sunday, spend it at Cicada Market with art, live music and street food.

Before you go: pick up snacks and gifts in town, then check out and head home with an easy mind.
